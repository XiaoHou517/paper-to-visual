## Artwork and research content

Cover and poster illustrations were generated with the host's image-generation tool as decorative conceptual imagery. They are not photographs of the authors' devices, experimentally measured signals, or verified engineering drawings.

The content-slide screenshots contain research summaries and presenter analysis with source identification. Research papers and their original figures remain attributable to their respective authors and rights holders. The source PDFs, full decks containing original paper figures, and third-party style-reference screenshots are not included in this repository.

The examples retain their original Chinese presentation language. All Markdown documentation and skill instructions in this repository are written in English. Request English artifact text explicitly when needed.
